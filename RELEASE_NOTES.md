## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-27

- Add a draggable minimap button. Left-click opens Quest Finder, shift-left-click toggles the last route, and right-click opens addon settings. Hide it in **Interface** settings. Quest Finder is also draggable by its header and remembers its position; `/fqp finderreset` returns it to the screen center.
- Quest Finder shows a per-zone Loremaster progress bar for the current or selected zone, calculated from eligible quests assigned to the zone, including chain steps with no map pin, and your completed quests. Repeatable, monthly, seasonal, and profession quests are excluded.
- Add a show-all option to every Quest Finder tab. **Show all breadcrumbs** adds completed and in-log breadcrumbs, **Show all zone quests** lists every Loremaster quest assigned to the zone, and **Show all dungeon quests** adds completed and unavailable dungeon quests and chain steps.
- Add an **Auto route** window for a selected zone or dungeon. It orders the quests and their required chains, deduplicates shared steps, and shows the current **Accept**, **Complete**, or **Turn in** step with a pin. Zone routes use the Loremaster quest list and source-quest prerequisites. The route re-evaluates after each quest event, zone change, and quest-giver interaction, hides completed steps, and supports Skip and Reset. Escape leaves the route window open; use its close button or shift-left-click on the minimap button to hide it.
- Add **Quest Finder** with breadcrumbs for the current or selected zone, quests available in a zone, and dungeon quests with their start zone and full required pre-quest chains. Breadcrumbs are matched by the zone they lead to, so a capital-city breadcrumb for the Plaguelands appears under the Plaguelands, breadcrumbs whose follow-up is already complete or in the log are hidden, and alternate capital versions are grouped into one row with every start city and a start-zone chooser. A search box filters the current view. Zone selectors use Leatrix Maps' zone levels and dungeon selectors use the journal's dungeon levels. The **All dungeons** view shows acceptable quests with an available-versus-total count; clicking a dungeon opens its full list, including quests you cannot pick up yet, with status labels. Availability uses the player's level and quest prerequisites, not the map pin level filters. Opposite-faction, wrong-race, and wrong-class quests and chain steps stay hidden. Quests that start inside a dungeon are tagged **In dungeon** and do not offer a map pin. Filter by dungeon and select a dungeon header to expand its chain. Click a row to set a Blizzard map pin from any zone. The breadcrumb and available-quest lists use the same filters as the map pins.
- Mark dungeon chain pre-quests as dungeon quests from ForeverDungeonJournal data, so outdoor steps that unlock a dungeon use the dungeon pin color and filter.
- Add a keybind that uses the special item on the tracked quest.
- Turn Questie's plain-text chat links into the game's own yellow clickable quest links. Existing links and clients with Questie loaded are left alone. Toggle it with `/fqp chatlinks`.
- The beta tracker's **All quests** view now includes every quest in the log, not only Blizzard's watched quests. **Tracked only** keeps the old view.
- Move pin filters back into Blizzard's world map tracking menu.
- Give book-only pins their own size slider, defaulting to 75% of a quest pin.
- Run QuestieDB updates Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
